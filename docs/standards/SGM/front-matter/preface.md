# Preface

> **Informative Notice**: This Preface is informative and does not contain normative requirements.

## 0.1 Introduction
The Standards Governance Model (SGM) establishes the constitutional framework for how standards and knowledge packages are authored, reviewed, published, and maintained within the SHRS ecosystem.

## 0.2 Purpose and Intended Use
The primary goal of this repository is to demonstrate self-governance. The repository itself acts as the living proof of the standards it defines. It is intended for use by system architects, technical writers, and governance bodies who wish to implement strict, immutable, and traceable knowledge management practices.

## 0.3 Audience
This document is intended for:
- Standards Maintainers and Editors
- Technical Authors drafting new packages
- Governance Board Members (when formally established)

## 0.4 Relationship with Future Standards
The SGM is the foundational layer. Future standards such as the Standards Engineering Guide (SEG), Standard Architecture Objects (SAO), and System Requirement Specifications (SRS) SHALL inherit their governance, publication, and structural rules from the SGM.
