# Annex A — Glossary

## Status

**Draft**

This glossary defines the canonical terminology used throughout the SHRS Standards Governance Model (SGM).

All capitalized defined terms used within this standard SHALL be interpreted according to the definitions contained in this annex.

---

## TERM-001 — Standard

**Definition**

A normative document approved through the SHRS governance process.

---

## TERM-002 — Edition

**Definition**

An approved publication baseline of a standard.

---

## TERM-003 — Version

**Definition**

A unique identifier assigned to a controlled state of a standard.

---

## TERM-004 — Revision

**Definition**

A controlled modification made to a published or working standard.

---

## TERM-005 — Amendment

**Definition**

A formally approved change that modifies part of an existing standard.

---

## TERM-006 — Corrigendum

**Definition**

A formally approved correction addressing errors without changing the intended normative meaning.

---

## TERM-007 — Canonical Repository

**Definition**

The authoritative repository containing the canonical source and governance evidence for SHRS standards.

---

## TERM-008 — Canonical Source

**Definition**

The approved authoritative source from which official publication artifacts are generated.

---

## TERM-009 — Standards Maintainer

**Definition**

The governance role responsible for maintaining the canonical repository and coordinating standards development.

---

*End of Annex A (Draft)*
