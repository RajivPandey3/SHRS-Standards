# Annex A — Glossary

## Status

**Draft**

This glossary defines the canonical terminology used throughout the SHRS Standards Governance Model (SGM).

All capitalized defined terms used within this standard SHALL be interpreted according to the definitions contained in this annex.

---

## TERM-001 — Standard

**Definition**

A normative document approved through the SHRS governance process.

---

## TERM-002 — Edition

**Definition**

An approved publication baseline of a standard.

---

## TERM-003 — Version

**Definition**

A unique identifier assigned to a controlled state of a standard.

---

## TERM-004 — Revision

**Definition**

A controlled modification made to a published or working standard.

---

## TERM-005 — Amendment

**Definition**

A formally approved change that modifies part of an existing standard.

---

## TERM-006 — Corrigendum

**Definition**

A formally approved correction addressing errors without changing the intended normative meaning.

---

## TERM-007 — Canonical Repository

**Definition**

The authoritative repository containing the canonical source and governance evidence for SHRS standards.

---

## TERM-008 — Canonical Source

**Definition**

The approved authoritative source from which official publication artifacts are generated.

---

## TERM-009 — Standards Maintainer

**Definition**

The governance role responsible for maintaining the canonical repository and coordinating standards development.

---

## TERM-010 — Governance Decision

**Definition**

A formal resolution made by an Approval Authority or Publication Authority regarding the status or content of a Standard.

---

## TERM-011 — Governance Record

**Definition**

An immutable record documenting a Governance Decision, Change Request, or Work Order.

---

## TERM-012 — Governance Evidence

**Definition**

The collection of artifacts, reviews, and formal approvals that justify a Governance Decision and are stored in the Canonical Repository.

---

## TERM-013 — Publication Artifact

**Definition**

Any generated document (e.g., PDF, HTML, Markdown) representing the approved Standard, derived strictly from the Canonical Source.

---

## TERM-014 — Release

**Definition**

The formal act of making a new Edition, Version, or Revision of a Standard available to its intended audience after passing the Publication Gate.

---

## TERM-015 — Work Order

**Definition**

A formal authorization that initiates drafting or modifications for a Standard.

---

## TERM-016 — Change Request

**Definition**

A formal proposal outlining specific modifications to be made to a Standard.

---

## TERM-017 — Lifecycle State

**Definition**

The current controlled status of a Standard (e.g., Draft, Under Review, Approved, Published, Deprecated).

---

## TERM-018 — Governance Gate

**Definition**

A mandatory checkpoint in the standards lifecycle that requires a formal Governance Decision to pass.

---

## TERM-019 — Publication Gate

**Definition**

The final Governance Gate where the Publication Authority verifies all Governance Evidence before a Release.

---

## TERM-020 — Approval Authority

**Definition**

The governance body or role responsible for evaluating and making Governance Decisions on normative changes.

---

## TERM-021 — Review Record

**Definition**

A documented outcome of peer reviews or evaluations conducted by an Approval Authority.

---

## TERM-022 — Publication Authority

**Definition**

The governance role responsible for overseeing the final Release of a Standard and verifying Governance Evidence at the Publication Gate.

---

*End of Annex A (Draft)*
