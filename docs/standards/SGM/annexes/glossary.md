# Annex A — Glossary

This glossary defines the canonical terminology used throughout the Standards Governance Model (SGM) and the wider SHRS repository.

## A.1 Terms and Definitions

### [TERM-001] Canonical Source
**Definition:** The authoritative version of a document or standard stored in the primary `main` branch of the SHRS repository.
**Used in:** Chapter 1, Chapter 2, Chapter 5

### [TERM-002] Governance Evidence
**Definition:** The collection of all artifacts, communications, and formal approvals that justify a Governance Decision.
**Used in:** Chapter 3, Chapter 6

### [TERM-003] Publication Artifact
**Definition:** A generated document (PDF, HTML, MkDocs) derived strictly from the Canonical Source.
**Used in:** Chapter 5

### [TERM-004] Release
**Definition:** The formal act of publishing a new Edition, Version, or Revision of a Standard.
**Used in:** Chapter 5

### [TERM-005] Standards Maintainer
**Definition:** The primary role responsible for day-to-day coordination and ensuring changes adhere to the SGM.
**Used in:** Chapter 3, Front Matter
