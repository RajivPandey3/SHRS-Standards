# Chapter 6 — Governance Records

A robust Standard model requires impeccable record-keeping. Every Governance Decision SHALL be permanently recorded and verifiable.

## 6.1 Governance Evidence
Governance Evidence is the collection of all artifacts, communications, and formal approvals that justify a Governance Decision. This evidence SHALL be preserved in the Canonical Repository alongside the Standard itself.

## 6.2 Record Types
The following are recognized as official Governance Records:
- **Work Orders**: Formal authorizations to initiate work or modifications.
- **Change Requests**: Proposals outlining specific modifications to be made.
- **Review Records**: Documented outcomes of peer reviews or evaluations by the Approval Authority.

## 6.3 Traceability and Auditability
Every requirement, modification, and governance decision SHALL be fully traceable. 
- The Canonical Repository SHALL maintain explicit linkage between records using the [Requirement Traceability Matrix](../annexes/requirement-traceability-matrix.md).
- Internal or external audits SHALL rely on the Repository as the definitive source of truth for all evidence.

## 6.4 Immutability
Once a Governance Record is finalized and signed off by the relevant Governance Role, it SHALL NOT be altered or deleted. Any corrections SHALL be issued as a new, linked Governance Record.
