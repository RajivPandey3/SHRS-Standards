# Chapter 2 — Governance Principles

The SHRS Standards Governance Model is built upon the following core principles to ensure integrity, consistency, and reliability in all normative publications.

## 2.1 Traceability
Every Revision, Amendment, or Corrigendum applied to a Standard SHALL be fully traceable. All changes MUST link back to a Governance Record and approved Work Order. 

## 2.2 Canonical Source of Truth
All official Publication Artifacts SHALL be derived exclusively from a single Canonical Source located within the Canonical Repository. Manual modifications to Publication Artifacts outside this repository are strictly forbidden.

## 2.3 Transparent Governance
The lifecycle of a Standard, including its Governance Gates and Publication Gates, SHALL be visible and auditable. Governance Decisions MUST be documented as Governance Evidence (see [Chapter 5](governance-records.md)).

## 2.4 Controlled Revisions
No Standard SHALL be modified without passing through an approved Lifecycle State change. Every Edition or Version MUST represent a stable and approved baseline.
