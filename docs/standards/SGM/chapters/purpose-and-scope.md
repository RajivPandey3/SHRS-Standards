# Chapter 1 — Purpose and Scope

## 1.1 Purpose
The purpose of the Standards Governance Model (SGM) is to establish a rigorous, transparent, and reproducible framework for the creation, maintenance, and publication of SHRS Standards. It ensures that all Standards undergo a controlled Lifecycle State transition, maintaining high quality and clear traceability.

## 1.2 Scope
This governance model applies to:
- All normative documents and Publication Artifacts published under the SHRS banner.
- The processes governing the approval, revision, and deprecation of Standards.
- The roles and responsibilities of the Standards Maintainer and other Governance Roles involved in the lifecycle.

## 1.3 Exclusions
This document does not cover the technical content or specific implementation guidelines of the individual Standards themselves, but rather the governance process surrounding them.
