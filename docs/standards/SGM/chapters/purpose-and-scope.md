# Chapter 1: Purpose & Scope

## 1.1 Purpose
The purpose of the Standards Governance Model (SGM) is to establish the constitutional framework for how standards and knowledge packages are authored, reviewed, published, and maintained within the SHRS ecosystem. It ensures that all structural and semantic rules are strictly enforced.

## 1.2 Scope
This standard covers:
- The lifecycle of all SHRS documentation and Knowledge Packages.
- The governance structure and roles responsible for maintaining the canonical repository.
- The processes for proposing, reviewing, and approving changes.
- The documentation of governance records and decisions.

### 1.2.1 Exclusions
This standard does not cover the technical implementation of the Knowledge Packages (which is covered by KRA and KAM baselines).

## 1.3 Applicability
The rules and guidelines defined in this SGM SHALL apply to all authors, maintainers, and reviewers interacting with the SHRS repository. 

## 1.4 Objectives
1. **Immutability:** Ensure released standards are permanently traceable.
2. **Transparency:** Maintain open records of all governance decisions.
3. **Consistency:** Enforce a unified editorial and structural format.

## 1.5 Conformance
A Knowledge Package or Standard is considered conformant if it strictly follows the lifecycles and rules defined herein. Non-conformant submissions SHALL be rejected at the governance gates.
