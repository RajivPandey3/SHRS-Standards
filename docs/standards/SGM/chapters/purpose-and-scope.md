# Chapter 1 — Purpose, Scope, Applicability, Normative References and Definitions

## 1.1 Purpose

The purpose of the SHRS Standards Governance Model (SGM) is to establish a uniform, transparent, and auditable governance framework for the development, review, approval, publication, maintenance, and evolution of standards within the Standardized Hierarchical Repository System (SHRS).

This chapter defines the constitutional foundation of the governance model and specifies the principles under which every normative SHRS standard SHALL be created and maintained.

---

## 1.2 Scope

This standard specifies the governance requirements applicable to standards engineering activities conducted within SHRS.

The scope of this standard includes:

- governance principles;
- governance roles and responsibilities;
- standards lifecycle management;
- review and approval processes;
- release governance;
- change governance;
- publication governance;
- governance records; and
- long-term maintenance of standards.

This standard does not define implementation-specific software architectures, repository tooling, or project-specific engineering procedures unless explicitly identified as normative.

---

## 1.3 Applicability

This standard applies to:

- all normative standards published under SHRS;
- all standards committees operating within SHRS;
- editors responsible for drafting standards;
- reviewers performing technical or editorial reviews;
- contributors authorized to modify standards; and
- governance bodies responsible for approval and publication.

Organizations adopting SHRS MAY define supplementary governance procedures provided that such procedures do not conflict with this standard.

---

## 1.4 Objectives

The objectives of this standard are to:

- establish a consistent governance framework;
- ensure traceability of all normative changes;
- maintain editorial consistency across standards;
- define clear governance responsibilities;
- support reproducible publication processes;
- preserve the integrity of standards throughout their lifecycle; and
- enable long-term maintainability of the SHRS standards ecosystem.

---

## 1.5 Design Principles

The governance model defined by this standard is founded upon the following principles:

- transparency;
- accountability;
- traceability;
- consistency;
- reproducibility;
- editorial independence;
- version integrity; and
- controlled evolution.

All governance activities SHOULD preserve these principles unless formally superseded by an approved governance decision.

---

## 1.6 Conformance

Conformance with this standard SHALL be evaluated solely against the normative requirements contained herein.

Informative notes, examples, guidance, and informative annexes SHALL NOT introduce mandatory obligations unless explicitly designated as normative.

Any document claiming conformance with SGM SHALL satisfy all applicable mandatory requirements.

---

## 1.7 Normative References

The following documents are considered normative references for this standard:

- RFC 2119 — Key words for use in RFCs to Indicate Requirement Levels.
- RFC 8174 — Ambiguity of Uppercase vs Lowercase Requirement Keywords.
- ISO/IEC Directives, Part 2 — Principles and Rules for the Structure and Drafting of International Standards.

Additional normative references MAY be incorporated through future approved revisions.

---

## 1.8 Terms and Definitions

For the purposes of this standard, the following definitions apply.

### Standard

A normative document approved through the SHRS governance process.

### Edition

A published baseline representing an approved release of a standard.

### Revision

A controlled modification made to a standard following the governance process.

### Work Order

A formally authorized engineering activity with defined objectives, deliverables, and acceptance criteria.

### Canonical Source

The authoritative repository source from which all publication artifacts are generated.

### Publication Artifact

A generated representation of the canonical source intended for distribution.

---

## 1.9 Interpretation

Where ambiguity exists, interpretation SHALL follow the following order of precedence:

1. Normative requirements.
2. Terms and definitions.
3. Governance principles.
4. Approved governance decisions.
5. Informative guidance.

Interpretations SHALL preserve the intent of this standard while minimizing ambiguity and ensuring consistency.

---

## 1.10 Compliance Statement

Any standard claiming compliance with the SHRS Standards Governance Model SHALL:

- comply with all applicable normative requirements;
- maintain a complete and traceable revision history;
- preserve the integrity of the canonical repository source;
- support independent technical and editorial review;
- maintain publication traceability; and
- document all approved deviations through formal governance decisions.

Failure to satisfy mandatory requirements SHALL constitute non-conformance unless an exception has been formally approved through the SHRS governance process.
