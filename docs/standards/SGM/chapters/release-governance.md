# Chapter 5 — Publication Governance

## 5.1 Purpose

This chapter establishes the constitutional governance framework for the publication of SHRS standards.

It defines the authority, requirements, controls, and governance mechanisms governing the publication of approved standards and the generation of official publication artifacts.

Publication SHALL occur only after successful completion of the governance lifecycle established by this standard.

---

## 5.2 Publication Principles

Publication governance SHALL be governed by the following principles:

- authenticity;
- integrity;
- traceability;
- reproducibility;
- controlled publication; and
- preservation of the canonical source.

No publication activity SHALL compromise the constitutional integrity of a standard.

---

## 5.3 Publication Authority

Publication SHALL be authorized only by the designated Publication Authority.

The Publication Authority SHALL verify that:

- all mandatory governance reviews have been completed;
- approval has been formally granted;
- governance records are complete;
- publication metadata is complete; and
- the canonical source has been approved.

Publication without authorization SHALL be considered invalid.

---

## 5.4 Publication Gates

Every publication SHALL pass through mandatory publication gates.

The minimum publication gates are:

- Approval Verification;
- Canonical Source Verification;
- Metadata Verification;
- Traceability Verification; and
- Publication Authorization.

Failure to satisfy any mandatory publication gate SHALL prevent publication.

---

## 5.5 Canonical Publication

The approved canonical repository source SHALL constitute the only authoritative source from which official publications are generated.

Generated publication artifacts SHALL faithfully represent the approved canonical source.

No publication artifact SHALL become the canonical source.

Manual modification of published artifacts SHALL NOT modify the approved standard.

---

## 5.6 Publication Artifacts

Publication MAY generate one or more official publication artifacts.

Examples include:

- Markdown;
- PDF;
- HTML; and
- other approved publication formats.

Every publication artifact SHALL:

- identify the approved edition;
- identify the publication version;
- preserve publication metadata; and
- remain traceable to the canonical source.

---

## 5.7 Release Management

Release management SHALL govern the controlled distribution of approved publication artifacts.

A release SHALL identify:

- release identifier;
- publication version;
- applicable edition;
- release date;
- included publication artifacts; and
- associated governance records.

Release activities SHALL NOT modify normative content.

---

## 5.8 Edition Baselines

Every approved edition SHALL establish a publication baseline.

Published baselines SHALL remain immutable.

Subsequent revisions SHALL establish new publication baselines without altering previously approved editions.

---

## 5.9 Publication Integrity

Publication integrity SHALL ensure that official publications accurately represent the approved canonical source.

Publication governance SHALL prevent:

- unauthorized publication;
- undocumented modification;
- inconsistent publication artifacts; and
- loss of publication traceability.

---

## 5.10 Post-Publication Governance

Following publication, governance SHALL continue throughout the maintenance lifecycle.

Post-publication governance MAY include:

- amendments;
- corrigenda;
- maintenance revisions;
- deprecation; and
- withdrawal.

All post-publication activities SHALL comply with the lifecycle governance established by Chapter 4.

---

## 5.11 Compliance

Organizations implementing SHRS SHALL ensure that publication governance complies with the constitutional requirements established by this chapter.

Failure to satisfy mandatory publication requirements SHALL constitute non-conformance.

---

**End of Chapter 5**
