# Article 3: Architectural Humility (SEC-021)

## 3.1 The Limit of Planning
No architecture is perfect until it survives contact with reality. Theoretical planning has diminishing returns. An ecosystem cannot mature without the friction of real-world implementation.

## 3.2 The Humility Rule (SEC-021)
**Every architectural decision is assumed correct only until implementation provides better evidence.**

## 3.3 The Supremacy of Evidence
Architecture serves engineering. Engineering does not serve architecture. 
If implementation proves that a foundational rule is creating unnecessary friction, reducing maintainability, or failing the "2036 Test", the implementation wins. The architecture MUST be revised based on that evidence.

This principle prevents dogma and ensures the ecosystem evolves safely without collapsing under its own rigid complexity.
