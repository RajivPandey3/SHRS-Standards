# SHRS Ecosystem Constitution (SEC)

**Edition:** 1  
**Status:** Working Draft (WD)

The SHRS Ecosystem Constitution (SEC) is the supreme legal document governing the entire engineering ecosystem. It derives its authority directly from the Founding Charter (ADR-0001) and the SHRS Vision (ADR-0003).
